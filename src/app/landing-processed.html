FAQ

Questions sales leaders ask before switching CRM.

What is moonCliq CRM?+

moonCliq CRM is a sales CRM for growing businesses. It helps your team follow a clear sales process, never miss a follow-up and talk to customers on official WhatsApp, all in one app.

What are Process Rules in moonCliq CRM?+

In a normal pipeline, anyone can move a deal to any stage. With Process Rules, each stage has a checklist, such as required documents, details to fill and manager approval, and the deal cannot move ahead until it is done.

Does moonCliq CRM work with official WhatsApp?+

Yes. moonCliq CRM connects directly to official WhatsApp with no markup on messages. You can send quotations, payment links and dispatch updates to your customers from the CRM.

Can I customise the CRM for my industry?+

Yes. Add your own sections such as Site Visits, Invoices, Machines or Service Contracts, each with its own stages and fields, and set up automations that fit your business.

Is moonCliq CRM compliant with India's DPDP Act 2023?+

Can it connect with Tally, Razorpay or SAP?+

Yes. moonCliq CRM can connect with tools like Razorpay, Tally, SAP or your own software to keep data in sync automatically.
